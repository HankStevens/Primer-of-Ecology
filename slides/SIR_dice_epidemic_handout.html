Dice Epidemic: Density- vs. Frequency-Dependent Transmission

Ch. 12: SIR disease models · Work in groups of 3–4 · You need two dice (row, column) and a pencil
Names:
Our population size: N = ______  (8, 16, or 32)
Our rule (circle):  DD  /  FD
  1. Set up. Roll row & column N times; draw a dot in each cell (re-roll occupied cells). Pick one dot as the first infected and write 0 in it (infected in round 0). All other dots are S.
  2. Each round t = 1, 2, 3, … every infectious individual makes 2 contact attempts by rolling a cell. If the cell holds an S, write t in it (newly infected; it starts infecting next round). A hit on I or R does nothing.
    Empty cell or yourself?  DD: the contact is wasted.   FD: re-roll until you hit another individual.
  3. Recovery. An individual infected in round k is infectious in rounds k+1 and k+2, then recovers: at the end of round t, put an X through every cell marked t−2 (now R).
  4. Record new infections, S, I, and R at the end of each round. Stop when I = 0. Then plot S and I versus t (use different symbols).
